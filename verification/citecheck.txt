(one line per cited source: "- S#: publisher, "title", its links"). "script.md:N" is a line of script/script.md.
It fetches every link, then shows for each claim the two sentences of the source that share the most words with it.
NO MATCH means no sentence shares enough of the claim's words; it flags paraphrase and numbers spoken as words as
well as real gaps, so a flag is for a person to read, not a verdict. TITLE flags here come mostly from the generated
list joining several titles of one source. NOT CHECKED means the fetch failed. The verdicts are in claims/. One quoted
organisation phone number is left out.
